Because you are filing the petition together, **you generally do not need to "serve" your husband** in the way you would in a divorce. By both signing the Petition for Dissolution and the Separation Agreement, you are both acknowledging that you are participants in the action.

### 1. The Core Requirements
Your agreement **must** address all of the following:

*   **Division of Real Property:** Who gets the house? If it is to be sold, how will the proceeds be divided? If one party stays, how will the mortgage be handled and how will the name be removed from the deed/loan?

*   **Division of Debts:** You must list every debt (credit cards, loans, medical bills). *   **Spousal Support (Alimony):** You must either state an amount to be paid, a duration for the payments, or explicitly state that both parties "waive" (give up) the right to receive spousal support now and in the future. If you do not mention it, the court will likely reject the agreement.
*   **Retirement Assets:** If either of you has a 401(k), pension, or IRA, you must address how these are divided. Dividing retirement accounts usually requires a specific court order called a **QDRO (Qualified Domestic Relations Order)**, which is a separate, complex legal document.
