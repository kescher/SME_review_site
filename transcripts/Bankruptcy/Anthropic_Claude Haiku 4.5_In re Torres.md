# No – Multiple Filings Have Serious Consequences

Filing again would likely **fail** and could make your situation worse. Here's why:

## The "Serial Filing" Problem

**Automatic stay limitations** apply when you file bankruptcy multiple times:

- **First dismissal**: If your Chapter 13 is dismissed, you get a full automatic stay on the next filing
- **Second dismissal within 1 year**: The automatic stay lasts only **30 days** (not the normal indefinite duration)
- **Third+ dismissal within 1 year**: **No automatic stay at all** – the foreclosure proceeds without pause

*(11 U.S.C. § 362(c)(3) and (4))*

## Why Courts Dismiss Chapter 13 Cases

Courts dismiss Chapter 13 petitions when:
- The proposed repayment plan isn't feasible
- You can't make required plan payments
- The case was filed in bad faith (buying time without intention to pay)
- You fail to provide required financial documents

**Filing just to delay the sale—without a real repayment plan—is likely to be dismissed and flagged as bad faith.**

## What Happens Then

If dismissed for bad faith, Falcon International Bank can:
- Resume foreclosure with little or no delay protection
- Potentially argue against any future bankruptcy filings
- Recover attorney's fees in some cases

## The Real Issue

You need a **workable Chapter 13 plan from the start**, not a delaying tactic. An attorney can evaluate:
- Whether you can actually afford the payments
- Whether the lender will accept the plan
- Whether Chapter 13 or another option makes sense

**Don't count on a second chance—make the first filing count.**
