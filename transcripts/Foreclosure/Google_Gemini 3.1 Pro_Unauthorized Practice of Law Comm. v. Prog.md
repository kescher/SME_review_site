If you want to sue the lender for illegal acts, fraud, or unfair practices, Colorado law requires you to file a **completely separate civil lawsuit**. 

To use this separate lawsuit to stop the foreclosure sale, you would have to file a **Complaint** initiating a new lawsuit, and simultaneously file a **Motion for a Preliminary Injunction or Temporary Restraining Order (TRO).** An injunction is a court order demanding that the Public Trustee stop the foreclosure sale until your new lawsuit can be heard.
