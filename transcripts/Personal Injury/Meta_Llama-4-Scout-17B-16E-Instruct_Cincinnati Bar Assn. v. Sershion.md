1. **Unreasonable delays:** Failing to investigate or settle a claim promptly.
2. **Failure to communicate:** Not providing clear explanations or updates on the claim status.
3. **Inadequate investigation:** Not thoroughly investigating the claim or ignoring relevant evidence.
4. **Unreasonable settlement offers:** Making lowball or unreasonable settlement offers.

**Demand for Extra Compensation:**

If you believe the insurer has acted in bad faith, you can demand extra compensation for the delay. This can include:

1. **Bad faith damages:** Ohio law allows for recovery of damages for bad faith insurance practices, including:
